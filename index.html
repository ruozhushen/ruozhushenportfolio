Building Resilient Supply Chains for Natural Disasters

Analyzed the impact of natural disasters on global supply chains and examined how unexpected disruptions can affect suppliers, inventory, transportation, production, and overall business operations. Our team developed a disaster-resilience framework focused on risk identification, supplier diversification, inventory and capacity buffers, digital visibility, and recovery and communication planning. We also considered how businesses can prepare for disruptions, maintain operational continuity, and respond more effectively when supply chain risks occur. Through this project, I strengthened my understanding of supply chain risk management, strategic planning, and the importance of balancing efficiency with resilience. The team-based project also helped me develop stronger research, communication, presentation, and collaborative problem-solving skills.

RU Trading: Campus Second-Hand Marketplace

Completed a team-based business simulation focused on RU Trading, a hypothetical campus second-hand marketplace designed to provide students with a safe, affordable, and convenient way to buy and sell used goods. Our team developed a business plan by analyzing customer needs and value, establishing financial and strategic objectives, and designing key operational processes for the simulated business. We also considered employee engagement, process improvement, and potential domestic and international expansion strategies as part of the company's long-term development. The project required us to evaluate different business decisions and connect management concepts with realistic business situations. Through this simulation, I gained practical experience in business planning, strategic decision-making, teamwork, communication, and applying management concepts to a realistic business scenario.
